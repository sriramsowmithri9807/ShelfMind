ShelfMind: interactive 3D replay

What you are watching: a real run of ShelfMind with NVIDIA Nemotron (served by Nebius Token Factory) as the brain. The gantry restocks the shelves; a box slips out of the gripper and Nemotron replans; then a box is knocked off the shelf, the humanoid inspector finds it and Nemotron plans the repair. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and use the play bar to scrub.
